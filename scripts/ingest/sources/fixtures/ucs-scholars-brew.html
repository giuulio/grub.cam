Scholars Brew

Scholars Brew, formerly the West Café, is a vibrant and cosy space where great coffee meets good company.

Whether you are grabbing a quick bite between lectures, catching up with friends, or just in need of a quiet corner and a warm drink, you can expect freshly brewed coffee, delicious food and friendly faces.
