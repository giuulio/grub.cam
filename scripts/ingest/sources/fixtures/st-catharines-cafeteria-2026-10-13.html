Cafeteria

The Cafeteria in the Hall provides a wide range of dishes for breakfast, lunch and dinner every day, which you can eat in Hall or take back to your room (you are encouraged to bring your own reusable containers if you want takeaways), however we do offer take away containers at a small cost.

In addition to the hot main dishes you will find a fresh salad bar, homemade soup and bread, fruits, soft drinks and sweet treats available. If you have any special dietary requirements (e.g. gluten-free, lactose intolerance, vegan, nut allergies, Kosher, Halal etc.) please make yourself known to a supervisor on duty in the hall.
