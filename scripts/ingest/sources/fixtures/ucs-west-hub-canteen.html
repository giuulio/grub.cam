West Hub Canteen

 

The West Hub Canteen is the place to go for an exciting range of hot and cold food.

The menu includes main meals, offering plenty of delicious gluten-free and plant-based options. The weekly menu is published below.

The Canteen serves breakfast, lunch and afternoon snacks.
