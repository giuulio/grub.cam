## Formal
- **Type:** formal
- **Days:** usually Tue, Fri (term); dates in College Calendar ([formal halls](https://www.hughes.cam.ac.uk/student-centre/practical/dining/formal-halls/))
- **Time:** 19:00 drinks in Peter Richards Room; 19:30 dinner
- **Dress:** lounge suit (jacket and tie) or equivalent; no jeans, T-shirts, trainers; gowns optional (none lent)
- **Format:** waiter service; no high table, Fellows sit with students; themed formals through the year
- **Booking:** UPay (meal credit required); Tue formal by 10:00 the previous Fri; Fri formal by 10:00 the previous Tue; transfers via Conference Office by 14:30 the day before
- **Guests:** personal guests, only with their host
- **Cost:** £17 student; £20 guest
