## Formal
- **Type:** formal
- **Where:** Dining Hall or Combination Room
- **Days:** Tue, Fri (full term); Tue otherwise
- **Time:** 19:15 to ~21:45 (drinks reception, 1.5 h dinner, coffee, port)
- **Dress:** formal business attire (jacket and tie or equivalent); gowns optional; no jeans, trainers, shorts, hats
- **Format:** candlelit three courses; wine package (reception glass, two with dinner, port) or no-wine card; no devices or photos; "Music and Madeira" nights ([formal halls](https://www.wolfson.cam.ac.uk/college-life/food/formal-halls))
- **Booking:** [Dining Portal](https://formal.wolfson.cam.ac.uk/DiningPortal/) using University-card credit; closes 12:00 the day before; waiting list
- **Guests:** up to 3 non-Wolfson guests, accompanied; 18+; groups of 10–20 by permission (one per formal)
- **Cost:** with wine / without: student £28.00 / £17.80; student guest £31.56 / £21.36; non-student and their guests £41.70 / £31.50. Students get a free formal allowance
