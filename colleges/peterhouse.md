## Formal
- **Type:** formal
- **Days:** every evening in Full Term (dates on UPay)
- **Time:** 19:30 (Full Term); queue via the Servery from Old Court
- **Dress:** gowns for junior members (also at Super Halls)
- **Format:** served, candlelit three-course dinner; menus in petmenu `formal.json`; BYO 75cl wine (corkage £2) or buy from the College Bar
- **Booking:** UPay, opens a week ahead, closes 11:00 the day before; no changes after
- **Guests:** up to 2 per member; host must attend
- **Cost:** 2025/26: £9.50; guest surcharge £4.60 incl. VAT (2026/27 under review)
