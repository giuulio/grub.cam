## Formal
- **Type:** formal
- **Days:** Full Term, every evening except Wed; traditional Formal Mon, Fri, Sat, Sun; group/society bookings other nights
- **Time:** seated by 19:15; dinner 19:30; late arrivals refused
- **Dress:** gowns compulsory for members
- **Format:** served dinner in Hall; no alternative dishes; no toasts, singing or drinking games
- **Booking:** online, by 19:00 three days before; free cancellation to 18:45 the day before ([rules PDF, Nov 2025](https://apps.emma.cam.ac.uk/college/documents/pdfs/FORMALS.pdf))
- **Guests:** up to 4 per member on traditional nights; groups of up to 10 non-members booked 10 days ahead
- **Cost:** £13.10 (living in College) / £13.95 (living out); 1st guest £22.65, further guests £28.30 (Nov 2025 PDF)

## Notes
- Formals PDF is internally inconsistent: says group bookings on "Tuesdays and Saturdays" and later "Thursday and Saturday". Ask catering.
